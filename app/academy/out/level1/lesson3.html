Personal Information

We use personal information to introduce ourselves.

Important questions:

  • What is your name?
  • How old are you?
  • Where do you live?
  • What is your phone number?
  • What is your email address?

Example Answers

My name is David.
I am 15 years old.
I live in Kinshasa.
My phone number is 123456789.
My email is david@gmail.com
